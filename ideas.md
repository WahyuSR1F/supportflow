# SupportFlow design brief

## Theme options considered
1. **Signal desk** — a high-contrast operations console with electric cobalt states and warm paper surfaces. Probability: 0.06
2. **Soft control room** — quiet mineral neutrals with ink typography and citrus action accents. Probability: 0.05
3. **Midnight relay** — a dark command center with cool cyan telemetry. Probability: 0.04

## Committed direction: Signal desk

- **Design movement:** editorial operations software: dense enough for an inbox, calm enough for a support team to scan all day.
- **Core principles:** make state visible; keep the customer thread primary; treat AI context as evidence, not decoration; use color sparingly for urgency.
- **Color philosophy:** warm off-white canvas (#f6f7f4), graphite text (#191b1d), cobalt action blue (#4263eb), mint-green confirmation (#1ea67a), ember orange for escalations (#e87843), and pale blue-gray dividers.
- **Layout paradigm:** persistent workspace rail, compact command header, three-column inbox on desktop, stacked cards on smaller screens.
- **Signature elements:** cobalt vertical active rail, channel glyphs in tinted squares, confidence meter, source citations, compact avatar stack, and live handoff banner.
- **Interaction philosophy:** optimistic, direct, and legible. Buttons explain the next state: Take over, Return to AI, Resolve thread.
- **Animation:** restrained 140–180ms transitions, subtle pulse for live status, no looping decorative motion.
- **Typography system:** Inter for UI and body, with tight semibold headings and generous line-height for messages.
- **Brand essence:** support teams feel one step ahead because the right context is always visible.
- **Brand voice:** calm, operational, specific, quietly confident.
- **Wordmark/logo:** SupportFlow wordmark paired with a simple relay-loop mark: two interlocking cobalt shapes with a mint counter-shape.
- **Signature brand color:** cobalt blue #4263EB.
